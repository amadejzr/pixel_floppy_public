## Before You Write

Rootkeep is offline-first and has no accounts or servers.
This means:

- I **cannot access your data**
- I **cannot recover deleted data** unless you have an iCloud backup (iOS)
- I never see your contacts, notes, or AI conversations

So when reporting a problem, the more details you provide, the faster and more accurate the help will be.

---

## How to Report a Bug

When reporting an issue, please include:

1. **Short description**
   What happened? What did you expect to happen?

2. **Steps to reproduce**
   A simple list of how you got to the issue, e.g.:
   1. Open the app
   2. Add a contact
   3. Log an interaction
   4. The issue appears

3. **Device information**
   - Device model
   - OS version
   - Rootkeep version (shown at the bottom of Settings)

Tip: Settings → Diagnostics lets you share the on-device error log, which helps a lot.

If the advisor is not responding, first check your key and credit balance at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com).
